Privacy Policy — OncoSein

Last updated: September 4, 2026

OncoSein ("the App") is a free, non-profit educational tool developed by OncoDev to help clinicians navigate breast cancer diagnosis and management. This policy explains how we handle information within the App.

Information We Collect

OncoSein does not require account creation and does not collect personal health information about patients. Any data entered into the App for calculation or reference purposes (such as clinical parameters used in decision-support tools) is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Local Storage

The App may use secure local storage on your device to save your preferences and settings. This data remains on your device and is not shared with third parties.

Network Access

The App may access the internet to check connectivity status or to enable sharing and printing features you initiate (such as exporting a summary). We do not use this access to collect analytics or track your usage.

Permissions

OncoSein does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or media files.

Children's Privacy

OncoSein is intended for use by healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
