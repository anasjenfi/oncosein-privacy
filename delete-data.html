Data Deletion — OncoSein

OncoSein stores saved clinical cases locally and securely on your device only. No data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

How to delete your data

You can delete your data directly within the app at any time:

  • Delete a single saved case: Open the saved case and select "Delete."
  • Delete all saved data: Go to Settings within the app and select the option to reset or clear all saved cases.

Deleting data within the app is immediate and permanent, as no copy is retained on any server.
